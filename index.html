Key design decisions

Why Herald is different.

⚡ The Agent Calls You
The Proactive Event Monitor runs continuously on your Mac Mini, watching your academic context. When it detects a trigger — exam in 2 hours with no recent study activity, assignment deadline approaching — it fires an APNs push to your iPhone.

Your phone rings. Caller ID shows Herald. You pick up. The agent briefs you and waits for your response. You reply verbally. It acts — builds a study guide, sets a reminder, drafts an email. Fully local. Zero cloud.
🛡 Privacy Hierarchy
