⚡ The Wow Feature
The Proactive Event Engine monitors your academic context continuously. When it detects a trigger — exam in 2 hours with no recent study activity, assignment deadline approaching, unread professor message — it fires an APNs push to your iPhone.

Your phone rings. Caller ID shows Herald. You pick up. The agent briefs you in natural speech and waits for your response. You reply verbally. It acts — builds a study guide, sets a reminder, drafts an email. Fully local. Zero cloud.
